# Design Context — AI Sales Team

## Users

**Primary:** OpenCode hackathon judges (Revenue track). 3-5 min watch per submission, daylight viewing, high comparison fatigue.
**Secondary:** SDR team leads / solo founders doing outreach — evaluating whether a multi-agent pipeline beats their current manual-plus-AI workflow.

Their job: understand in 30 seconds that this is a **team of named specialists**, not a black-box AI email generator. Then remember it 60 minutes later among 30+ other submissions.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **Newsroom · Machine Shop · Insolent.**

- **Newsroom** — editorial gravity, headline-style typography, wire-service dispatch summaries. Built by someone who reads the news, not someone who watches SaaS screenshots.
- **Machine shop** — mono typography for every piece of pipeline machinery (IDs, timestamps, persona chips, stage names). Industrial directness.
- **Insolent** — ruthless about removing filler. Will reject a prospect, revise a draft, or send a terse "override?" chip without flinching. Not cute. Not apologetic.

Emotional goals: **Transparency** (every agent decision visible) and **Power** (you command the team).

## Aesthetic Direction

**Brutalist + Editorial hybrid, light theme.**

### Inherited base
From `/Users/ketankhairnar/_work/code/portfolio/tacit-web`:
- mono-first, black + white + **#ff6b35** (now OKLCH-corrected), radius 0, 1-2px black borders, density over air, left-aligned, no gradients, no shadows.

### Typography pairing (all NOT in impeccable reject list)

- **Display (prospect names, campaign headlines):** **Source Serif 4** — editorial slab-adjacent gravity. NOT Fraunces, NOT Playfair, NOT Cormorant.
- **Body (prose, descriptions):** **Literata** — book-feel readability. NOT Lora, NOT Newsreader.
- **Machinery (timestamps, persona IDs, code, stage labels, chips):** **Geist Mono** — clean, current, not reflex. NOT IBM Plex Mono, NOT Space Mono.

### Layout principles

- **Kanban columns NOT equal width.** Weighted by importance: `Discovered(sm) Scored(sm) Researched(md) Drafted(lg) Sent(sm)`. Drafted is where email content lives, it gets the room.
- **Editorial prospect headers** in detail view: name set in Source Serif 4 display, role/company in Geist Mono. Reads like a magazine profile.
- **Wire-service campaign summary** at top of board: `CAMPAIGN c_8b1cb9eb · 20 discovered · 4 scored ≥6/10 · 3 drafted · 1 sent at 15:50 IST`. One line, mono, left-aligned, no icons.

### Motion

Minimal. No entrance animations except one: new event in activity feed gets 1.5s left-border accent pulse, then fades. Nothing else moves on its own.

## Anti-references (explicit)

- Salesforce / HubSpot — boxy enterprise
- Notion / Linear — gray-on-gray SaaS
- Vercel dashboard — distinctive but consumed already
- "AI startup" — purple gradients, dark mode, glow
- Terminal cosplay — green-on-black, fake CLI
- Dashboard metrics — big-number cards with sparklines
- Slack clone — bubble chat aesthetic
- Notion empty state ("Your workspace is empty 🎨")

## Absolute bans (impeccable reject list)

- Fraunces, Newsreader, Lora, Crimson, Playfair, Cormorant, Syne, IBM Plex, Space Mono, Inter, DM Sans, Outfit, Jakarta, Instrument Sans/Serif
- Gradient text (`background-clip: text` + gradient)
- Side-stripe borders on cards/alerts (`border-left: >1px`)
- Icons above headings
- Glassmorphism
- Rounded corners (global `radius: 0`)
- Nested cards
- Modal dialogs (use slide-sheets instead)

## The unforgettable thing

**Handoff chips.** Every agent-to-agent transition is rendered as a named mono chip in the activity feed and inline on stage grids:

```
[Shruti → Vikram: override?]
[Esha → Siddharth: revise hook]
[Vikram: reject — Series D mismatch]
```

These are *not* decorative — they show the actual argument happening between specialists. Judges don't see "AI generated this." They see **a team arguing about quality.**

## Design Principles

1. **Every decision is visible.** No black box. Every score, revision, override is rendered as content.
2. **Mono is machinery. Serif is humans.** Personas, timestamps, chips, IDs = mono. Prospect names, headlines, quotes = serif.
3. **Asymmetry earns emphasis.** 5 equal columns = information architecture indifference. Weight columns by what's happening.
4. **Density over air.** White space is earned by varied rhythm, not applied as default "breathing room."
5. **One bold accent, rare.** Orange for CTAs, hot scores (≥8), active stages, new events. Everything else monochrome.
6. **Text does the work.** A well-set sentence beats an illustration. No hero images, no stock icons.

## Accessibility

- Min body text 13px (mono has large x-height, reads larger than same-px serif)
- Contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for body, ≥ 3:1 for large text
- All color info duplicated in shape/text (e.g., score 9/10 + orange dot, not dot alone)
- Keyboard nav: `[`/`]` between stage columns, `↑/↓` in column, `Enter` opens card
- `prefers-reduced-motion` → disable the 1.5s accent pulse
